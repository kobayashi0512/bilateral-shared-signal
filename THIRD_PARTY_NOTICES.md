# Third-party data and image notices

This repository contains a small number of illustrative source-dataset photographs inside the manuscript and Figure 1/Figure 4 packages. They are included under their source terms and are not a mirror of either dataset.

- **DeepDRiD** — four selected original fundus images in Figure 1, from one training patient, with two views for each eye. Source: [DeepDRiD official repository](https://github.com/deepdrdoc/DeepDRiD) and dataset paper [Liu et al., Patterns 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patter.2022.100512). The source repository identifies CC BY-SA 4.0. The examples are resized for figure layout and otherwise unmodified.
- **PAPILA** — selected original OD/OS image pairs in Figure 1 and Figure 4. Source: [PAPILA Figshare record](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/PAPILA/14798004) and [Kovalyk et al., Scientific Data 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01388-1). The Figshare record identifies GPL-3.0+. The examples are resized for figure layout and otherwise unmodified.
- **CFP–OCT** — no source photographs are included. The repository reviewed for this project did not state an image-reuse license.

The paper cites the dataset publications in its bibliography. Reusers must independently review the source license and attribution requirements. No general license is granted here for dataset images beyond the permissions of their original providers.
