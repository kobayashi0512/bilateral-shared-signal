Figure 1. Framework for conditional bilateral fundus correspondence. (A) DeepDRiD contributes 300 complete bilateral patients, with two views per eye; the four source photographs are illustrative. The patient-disjoint split is 210/46/44. (B) Same-patient left–right pairs are compared with cross-patient negatives; four cross-eye view combinations and same-eye repeat views audit view dependence. (C) Fixed ImageNet-pretrained ResNet-50 features are compared by cosine similarity; no representation is trained for this raw-feature measurement. (D) Negative assignment uses DR grade, exact grade × quality tier, and nearest measured quality within grade. Quality-composite differences of 0.1982, 0.0709, and 0.0240 indicate progressively tighter matching, not improving prediction. (E) DeepDRiD supplies internal evidence; CFP–OCT is a previously inspected development stress set; PAPILA is the locked external test of the frozen baseline only. Four DeepDRiD images and the PAPILA OD/OS pair are unmodified illustrative source photographs [10,11].
