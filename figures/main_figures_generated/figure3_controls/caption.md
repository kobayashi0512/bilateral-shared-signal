Figure 3 | DeepDRiD pair correspondence under progressively stricter negative-patient controls. (A) Full-cohort diagnostic analysis of 300 complete bilateral patients. Gray trajectories show five fixed negative-pair assignments; teal points and whiskers show mean ± sample standard deviation (SD) across those seeds. Offset gray whiskers show the conservative envelope from the minimum lower to the maximum upper bound of five separately computed 95% paired-patient bootstrap confidence intervals (3,000 resamples per seed); this envelope is not itself a pooled confidence interval. The AUROC axis is enlarged and does not include chance level 0.5. (B) Four left–right view combinations on 43 eligible held-out test patients, with DR-grade-matched negatives; whiskers are across-seed SD. (C) Mean absolute source–negative difference in a dimensionless, normalized quality-composite score (overall quality, clarity, field definition and inverse artifact score); lower means tighter matching. (D) Training-fitted linear probes evaluated on 44 test patients. DR is diabetic retinopathy; AUROC is area under the receiver operating characteristic curve; OvR is one-versus-rest. Probe performance indicates readable metadata, not causal leakage. Panel A is not a held-out learned-model result.
